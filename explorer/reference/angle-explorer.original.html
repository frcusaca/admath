What is this curve?

Across is the angle from the first vector to the second. A plus sign means a counterclockwise turn and a minus sign means clockwise. Up is the normalized inner product.

The curve is 1 when the two vectors point the same way, 0 at a right angle, and −1 when they point opposite ways. It is the same on the left and the right, so the inner product cannot tell a left turn from a right turn.

This curve has a name: the cosine. The notebook function angle_between runs it backwards, to turn a normalized inner product into an angle.
